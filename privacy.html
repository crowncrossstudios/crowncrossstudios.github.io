Privacy Policy

War Ladder  ·  Last updated: 31 July 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game War Ladder ("the Game"), published by Crown & Cross Studios ("we", "us"), handles information. It applies to the Android application with the package name com.crowncross.warladder.

The short version

War Ladder does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The Game requests no Android permissions, contains no advertising, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind. It does not require an internet connection to play.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect personal information, device identifiers, location data, contacts, photos, advertising IDs, or usage analytics. No information about you or your device is transmitted to us or to any third party by the Game.

Data stored on your device

The Game saves your progress locally on your own device using your device's standard application storage. This includes:

  • Campaign and ladder progress
  • Audio, display, and accessibility settings
  • Best scores and completion records
  • Whether you have purchased the full version

This information never leaves your device and is not accessible to us. Uninstalling the Game, or clearing its data through your device settings, permanently deletes it.

Purchases

The Game offers an optional one-time in-app purchase to unlock the full experience. All purchases are processed entirely by Google Play using Google Play's billing system. We never see, receive, or store your payment card details, billing address, or any other financial information. Google provides us only with anonymised, aggregated sales reports that cannot be used to identify an individual purchaser.

Google's handling of your purchase information is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Children

War Ladder is not directed at children under 13. Because the Game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects no personal information from children.

Third parties

The Game contains no third-party advertising networks, analytics SDKs, social media integrations, or crash-reporting services. The only third party involved is Google Play, and only when you choose to make a purchase or when Google Play checks your existing purchase status.

Security

Because no data is collected or transmitted, there is no data held by us that could be subject to a breach. Your saved progress is protected by your device's own operating system security.

Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete personal data we hold about you. As we hold none, there is nothing to access, correct, or delete. Your locally stored game progress is under your sole control and can be erased at any time by clearing the Game's data or uninstalling it.

Changes to this policy

If the Game's data practices change in a future version — for example, if online leaderboards are added — this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the "Last updated" date above will change. Continued use of the Game after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

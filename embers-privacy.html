Privacy Policy

Embers of the Twin Flame · Last updated 14 August 2026

This policy applies to Embers of the Twin Flame, published by Crown & Cross Studios (“we,” “us”).

The short version

We do not collect, store, or transmit personal information. There is no account, no sign-in, and no server of ours that receives data about you. The game runs entirely on your device.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, contacts, photos, location, device identifiers, or advertising IDs.

Data stored on your device

The game saves progress locally so you can continue where you left off. This may include:

  • Depth reached, Rite Wheel state, and unlocks
  • Run history and War Ladder results
  • Audio, display, and control settings

This data never leaves your device and we cannot read it. Uninstalling the game removes it; clearing the app's storage in your device settings resets it at any time.

Permissions

The game requests no sensitive permissions. It does not use the camera, microphone, location services, contacts, or your files.

Third parties

The game contains no advertising, no analytics, and no third-party trackers. We do not sell or share data, because we hold none.

Purchases, downloads, and updates are handled by Google Play under Google's privacy policy. We receive only aggregate, anonymous sales and install statistics from Google Play — never information that identifies you.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and collects no data from anyone, including children. If you believe a child has provided us personal information, contact us and we will act on it.

Your rights

Rights such as access, correction, and deletion under the GDPR, Québec's Law 25, and comparable laws apply to personal data a company holds. We hold none, so there is nothing for us to retrieve or erase. Your local save data stays under your control on your device.

Changes to this policy

If a future release changes what the game does with data, we will update this page and revise the date above before that release ships.

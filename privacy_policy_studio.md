# Privacy Policy — Crown & Cross Studios

Effective 6 September 2026. Applies to every Crown & Cross Studios game,
including **War of the Watchers**, **Embers of the Twin Flame** and
**Embers — The Outer Thorn**.

## We collect nothing

Our games do not gather, transmit or sell personal information, identifiers,
usage analytics, advertising IDs or diagnostics. There are no accounts, no
advertising, and no third-party analytics or tracking SDKs of any kind.

## Your save data stays on your device

Progress is stored locally by the app, in storage belonging to the app itself.
It is never uploaded and we never see it. Uninstalling the game deletes it.
Where a game offers an export feature, it writes a file only to a location you
choose, only when you ask it to.

## Purchases

Some of our games offer a single optional in-app purchase that unlocks the rest
of the game.

Purchases are handled entirely by **Google Play Billing**. Payment is made to
Google, not to us. We never see or receive your name, card details, address or
any other payment information. The game asks Google only one question — whether
this account owns the unlock — and receives only yes or no.

## Downloading additional content

Where a game delivers part of its content after purchase, that download is
served by **Google Play Asset Delivery**. The request goes to Google Play, on
your device, in the same way as installing the app. No data reaches us.

## Children

Our games are not directed at children under 13 and we knowingly collect no
data from anyone, of any age.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted at this address with
a new effective date.
